Trace — Privacy Policy

Effective 11 June 2026

Trace does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

What the app reads

All system statistics shown by Trace — CPU, memory, storage, battery, network throughput, IP address and uptime — are read locally from macOS and never leave your Mac. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

Network access

Trace's only network request is to the public macOS release feed at sofafeed.macadmins.io, used to tell you when a newer macOS version is available. This request sends no personal or device-identifying information.

Device information in the Support window

Details shown in the Support window (such as your Mac's model, serial number and local IP address) are displayed for your own use when contacting your IT helpdesk. They are placed on the clipboard only when you click "Copy for ticket", and are never transmitted by the app.

Managed (MDM) settings

If your organization deploys Trace, it may preconfigure helpdesk contact details and announcements via managed preferences. This is one-way configuration pushed to the app; Trace does not report anything back to your organization.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
