Trace

Your Mac's vital signs, always one glance away —
and everything IT support needs, one click away.

For macOS 26 or later · Free on the Mac App Store

What it does

Live in the menu bar

Stacked bars, equaliser columns, a percentage, a ring, or a scrolling CPU/memory graph — your choice, showing the metrics you pick.

One panel, everything

CPU, memory, storage, battery with 24-hour chart, network speed and IP, VPN status, and uptime.

Every tile is a shortcut

Rings open Activity Monitor. Storage, battery and Wi-Fi jump straight to the right System Settings pane.

Quiet alerts

Optional notifications when storage runs low or a restart is overdue. You set the thresholds.

Built for IT support

Model, serial, macOS build, IP and more — copied for a ticket in one click. MDM-configurable helpdesk links.

Desktop widget

Small and medium widgets keep your stats on the desktop. Private by design: Trace collects nothing.

Support

I launched Trace but no window appeared — where is it?

Trace lives in the menu bar, not the Dock. Look for the small stacked-bars icon near the right side of the menu bar and click it — or press ⌥ ⇧ T anywhere — to open the panel.

How do I change what the menu bar icon shows?

Open the panel → gear icon → Menu bar icon. Pick Bars, Columns, Percent, Ring or Graph, and tick which metrics the bars/columns styles include.

How do I add the desktop widget?

Right-click your desktop → Edit Widgets… → search "Trace" → add the small or medium widget. Run Trace at least once first so the widget has data.

Alerts aren't appearing

Check the toggles in Trace's Settings → Alerts, then make sure notifications are allowed in System Settings → Notifications → Trace.

Can I hide Apple's battery icon since Trace shows battery?

Yes — System Settings → Control Center → Battery → "Don't Show in Menu Bar".

How do I quit or relaunch Trace?

Quit from the power button at the bottom of the panel, or right-click the menu bar icon → Quit. Relaunch from your Applications folder, or enable "Launch at login" in Settings.

I'm an IT admin — can I preconfigure Trace for my organization?

Yes. Trace reads managed preferences (e.g. via Jamf's Application & Custom Settings) for helpdesk portal, email, phone, ticket-URL templates with device placeholders, announcement banners and launch-at-login. Contact us for the key reference.
