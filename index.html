Support

Questions, answered.

I launched Trace but no window appeared — where is it?
Trace lives in the menu bar, not the Dock. Look for the small stacked-bars icon near the right side of your menu bar and click it — or press ⌥ ⇧ T anywhere — to open the panel.
How do I change what the menu bar icon shows?
Open the panel → gear icon → Menu Bar. Pick Bars, Columns, Percent, Ring or Graph, and tick which metrics the bars and columns include.
How do I change the layout or colours of the panel?
Settings → Panel lets you switch the CPU & Memory gauges between rings, pies and bars, and show, hide or reorder every section. Settings → Appearance has eight themes plus fully custom per-metric colours.
How do I add the desktop widget?
Right-click your desktop → Edit Widgets… → search "Trace" → add the small, medium or large widget. Run Trace at least once first so the widget has data.
Alerts aren't appearing
Check the toggles in Trace's Settings → Alerts, then make sure notifications are allowed in System Settings → Notifications → Trace. Each alert also has a repeat frequency — from every 15 minutes to weekly, or "Once" until the condition clears.
What languages does Trace speak?
English, Spanish, German, French and Japanese — the app follows your macOS language setting automatically.
Can I hide Apple's battery icon since Trace shows battery?
Yes — System Settings → Control Center → Battery → "Don't Show in Menu Bar".
How do I quit or relaunch Trace?
Quit from the power button at the bottom of the panel, or right-click the menu bar icon → Quit. Relaunch from your Applications folder, or enable "Launch at login" in Settings.
I'm an IT admin — can I preconfigure Trace for my organization?
Yes. Trace reads managed preferences (e.g. via Jamf's Application & Custom Settings) for helpdesk portal, email, phone, ticket-URL templates with device placeholders, announcement banners and launch-at-login. The admin guide has the complete key reference, per-MDM walkthroughs, and ready-made downloads including a Jamf custom schema.
